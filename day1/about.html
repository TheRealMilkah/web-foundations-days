About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight web application designed to help you capture ideas quickly. It is built for students and professionals who want a simple, distraction-free place to store their thoughts.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Go to the Home page and enter a title for your note.
  2. Type your note content in the content area.
  3. Click Add Note to save it to your list.

Features

  • Fast and simple interface
  • Create and organize notes instantly
  • Works on all devices
  • Free to use offline
